### Mixing

Horizontal mixing: Seamless segway between songs using transitions from place to place (outside, village, house...)

Vertical mixing: Same melody with different instruments/style (outside field, outside canyon, outside glacier...)

Evolving: Instruments added as temple are progressed/in certain room

Other: instruments added in accordance of certain objects ?
