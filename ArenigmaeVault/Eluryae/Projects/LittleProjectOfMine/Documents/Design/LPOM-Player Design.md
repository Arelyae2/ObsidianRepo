## Ressources

The player has two main resources during the game, their **health**, and **money**. There is no endurance or magic system, we must keep the overall economic system as **simple** as we can.
### Health

The player's health will be represented by a heart system..
Mathematically, a full heart means 4 health point.

![[HeartContainerZeldaTP.png|150]]
The player will, in maximum, have 30 heart, so 120 health point in total.
They start with 4 heart (16 health point)
#### Drain
The player will lose health against enemies, environmental hazard...
There is no passive system that makes them lose their health.
![[Minecraft.png|550]]

If the player lose all their health, they get a Game Over, and go back to the previous save point.

#### Tap

They can gain their health back with:
- Health **potion** boughtable from merchant
- Health point from **dead enemies**
- Health point by **cutting grass**

#### Upgrade
The player can upgrade their maximum heart by collecting piece of it around the world, completing sidequest, or at the end of temples.

![[HeartContainer.png|550]]

---
### Money
The player can use **money** to buy items from merchants around the world. 
The money is made of **copper coin**, with each having a different value.

Each coin can **represent the face of a famous person** in the world.

- **Basic** coin - 1 unit (damaged, missing a piece)
- **Freshly** coin - 5 unit (solid copper coin)
- **Honored** coin - 10 unit (copper coin with some ornaments)
- **Precious** coin - 20 unit (copper coin with gems)
- **Silvery** coin - 50 unit (copper coin with gems and silver)
-  **Ornamented** coin - 100 unit (beautifully ornamented silver and copper coin with gems on the sides)
-  **Silver** coin - 200 unit (a fully silver coin with diamonds on the side)

There is also **rare iterations** of the coins for collectibles, see in [[LPOM-Side Quests#Rare silver coin|Rare Silver coins]]

![[SilverCoin.png|550]]
#### Drain
The player can loose money only by spending it, they don't loose money by dying, getting damaged, or anything.

Some place during the game will ask them for money in order to progress.

![[MerchantResidentEvil.png|550]]

#### Tap
The player can gain money by:
- Selling rare coins to the collectioner (see [[LPOM-Side Quests#Rare silver coin|Rare silver coin]])
- Cutting **grass**
- Opening **chests**
- From **dead enemies**

#### Upgrade
The player only has a **maximum** amount of coin they can carry.

They can upgrade it by doing **side quests** or buying it from merchants.

## Objects

### Gun

The player obtain a Gun during the first temple of the game.
#### General

![[AntiquePistol.png|400]]

The pistol fire **pellets** that can be bought from merchants or looted from dead enemies.
The pellets do **not do much damage**, the gameplay does not encourage it.

The player can only fire **one pellet at a time, and needs reloading**.

---

The pistol however, has several uses:
- Cutting **rope**
- **Interrupts** enemy attack
- **Blind** enemy if they get shot in the eye
- Light oil or torch **on fire**
---

To fire the gun, the player firstly has to aim it (see [[#Upgrade Gun]] for exceptions) using the *Aim Gun Input*, then fire with the *Fire input*.
[[LPOM-3C#Controls|Controls]]

If they **fire before they finished aiming**, the pistol is **stopped** and cannot be used for a few seconds.

---

During [[LPOM-3C#Targeting|Targeting]], they will aim **towards the targeted entity.** Otherwise, the camera will switch to a **shoulder angle** and a **crosshair** will appear. 
The player will then **aim with the right stick**.
#### Upgrade Gun

The general section described the **base uses** of the gun when the player obtains it for the first time.

They can however **upgrade** it for money in the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Riospe Town|Malenaian]] town.

![[OldPistols.png|400]]

The upgrades changes the appearance of the pistol.

The gun can be customisable for the player's taste.

##### Double cannon
The player can now fire **two pellets before reloading**.

---
##### Damage
The pellets now do **more damage**.

---
##### Draw speed
The player **aims faster**.

---
##### Damage
The pellets now do **more damage**.

---
##### Draw Speed
The player aim is now so fast it **doesn't require** them to use the *Aim input*, they just have to **press and release** the *Fire Input*

---
##### Double Grapling hook
See [[#Grapling Hook]], it is now doubled, giving the player the ability to move itself **without touching the ground**.

---
##### Explosive pellets
The player can now choose to fire explosive pellets, it asks for a **higher reloading time**. This allows to destroy damaged walls or rock.

They can **switch to normal pellets, or grapling hook** at any time, it doesn't replace the base pellets.

The **Explosive pellets** upgrade can be done **before** the **double Grapling hook**.

#### General
The player obtain a grappling hook during the game.
![[GrapplingHook.png|400]]

It is used as a way to go to **higher places** by grappling on given surfaces and objects., not for balancing. It can also be used for **suspension on moving platform**.

---

It can be used **in combat** for the player to grapple on enemies, and follow it with a sword hit.

---

The double grappling hook is a possible upgrade **after obtaining a second gun**. The player will then have the ability to grapple onto surfaces and objects, while **already being** suspended or grappled on.

![[GrapplingHookDragonZelda.png|600]]

The kitten takes refuge in their **backpack**, and can **hop on their shoulder** when walking, discussion, or during combat to **jump** at enemies.

![[MainCoonCatKitten.png|400]]

The cat also serve as a way to fetch particular objects unattainable for the player in temples or the world.

![[MainCoonCat.png|400]]

![[DoublePistols.png|400]]

## Movement
### General
The player base movement allow them to do several actions. They move in t**he direction wich the camera is looking** at.
#### Base
The player can **walk** by pushing their **joystick slightly**.
They can **run** as well, wich is the **base movement**.

![[CharacterControllerUnity.png|500]]

#### Roll
The player can **roll** and gain a **small speed boost**, and go back to their base speed rapidly.
![[RollingMechanic.png|500]]
#### Ledges
The player **automatically jump** from ledges.

Grab onto **ledges and move** on them left and right.
![[LedgesDetection.png|500]]


#### Climb
The player can **climb on given surfaces**, not all of them, but very specific one.
It is **not a core mechanic**, but something that allows for **verticality**.

![[ClimbingBOTW.png|500]]
They **automatically climb** on big enough object.

### Spirit

#### General

When in the Golden Corruption, the player is transformed into their spirit form. This form is vulnerable to the **Yellow Guardians**, and can be easily captured and emprisonned.

![[ForsakenFortressWindWaker.png|500]]

In spirit form, the player **cannot climb** or **jump** anymore. They are "attached" to the ground.

![[ShadowBG3.png|200]]

They can however ignore **cracked walls** and **fences**, completely **passing through** them.
They can still attack but do **less damage**.

#### Possessions
What the player can do however in spirit form, is **possess** other entities, **weak minded** living beings.

![[MarioOdysseyPossession.png|500]]

To possess an entity, it must be reachable in a **1 meter** circle, **not behind a wall,** and **targetable**.
They must then target it, and press the given input.

They must press the the **same input** to **get out** of a possession.

##### Guard
The guard/soldier of the place, they can go to places the [[#Citizen]] are not permitted to be and can attack. They **can't** take any **objects** in their hand.

However, they have to follow a very strict path to guard around a given zone, if the payer diverts from it, they will be chased by the **yellow guardians**.

![[CityGuard.png|300]]

---
##### Citizen
The simple citizen of the city, they can **carry objects**, and walk around more freely, but a lot of part of a given zone are **restricted** to them and they can't attack.

---

When possessing any animal, the player gets their **perks and disadvantage** as well.

---
##### Pests
- Rats - Can go in **narrow places**, but get **hunted by humans** and other animals (except fish and flying animals).
- Lizards - Can go in **narrow places** but get **hunted** by other animals.

---
##### Swimmers
- Fish - Can **swim in narrow** places, but get **hunted by alligators**.
- Alligator - Can **swim** and **attack** but cannot go in narrow places, get **hunted by guards**.

---
##### Flying
- Bat - They can see in the dark with **infrared vision** and fly, but only **upside down**, with inverted controllers. They get **hurt in the light of the sun**.
- Sun bird, they can fly across the map, but are **slow**, and get hurt at night.

---
##### Pets
- Dog - Can run but get hunted by guards
- Cat - Can run but get hunted by guards

---

## Combat

In their normal appearance, the player can use a **full range** of combat against their foes.

### Sword Combat

The sword combat is the base attack the player will use.

![[GustaveArenaGun.png|400]]

- *Base hit*
	- 1 hit
	- 2 hit combo
	- 3 hit combo
	- 4 hit combo
	
The **basic combo** of the player, simply pressing the *Attack Input*.
It holds a **combo of 4 hit**, the **last one doubling double damage**.

![[breathOfTheWildCombatZtargeting.png|500]]

- *Hit forward*
	- 1 hit
	- 2 hit combo
	- 3 hit combo
	- 4 hit combo
	
The same as the basic combo, but if the player is holding the forward input at the same time.


- *Roll into attack*
With an **[Input Buffer](https://wavu.wiki/t/Input_buffer)**, the player can **attack forward** just after the **end of a roll**, a single hit.


- *Jump attack*
When **targeting**, if the player is holding a *forward input*, and press the *roll input*, they do a jump attack toward the entity they target.


- *Walking Attack*
	- 1 hit
	- 2 hit
When **walking normally**, the player can do attack on the side **without changing or impacting their momentum**.

### Dodge

When in [[LPOM-3C#Targeting|Targeting]], the player can use the *roll input* to **dodge** enemy attacks and aim them with their *movement input*.

**Left and right** along the **horizontal axis**, and **backward** along the **negative vertical** axis.

One of the [[#Secret Moves]] the player can learn is if they perfectly dodge a hit at the right moment, they can immediately **counter attack with their sword** and one shot an enemy.

![[FlurryRushBotw.png|500]]

### Secret Moves

The player can encounter the **[[LPOM-Narrative Design#Forgotten Swordsman|Forgotten Swordsman]]** to learn new attack moves.

#### Circular Attack

By holding and releasing the *attack input*, the player does a circular attack. that can do damage to several enemies.
![[CircularAttack.png|500]]

---
#### Destabilization

By rapidly doing a **backward dodge** and **firing** their gun using only the *fire input* (no need for the aim), the player can directly **knock an enemy on the ground**.

---
#### Back Counter
By doing a rapid **dodge on either side**, and then an **attack**, the player will go **behind the enemy** and hit them, this is **useful against an enemy with a heavy shield**.

![[AnakinMoveGif.gif]]

---
#### Perfect Dodge
If the player perfectly dodge a hit at the right moment, they can immediately **counter attack with their sword** and one shot an enemy, or at least stun them/deal massive damage

![[FlurryRushBotw.png|500]]

---
#### Counter more enemies
This is an upgrade of the counter, the player will, **after** one shotting a **first** enemy, be given the ability to **fire** a shot to the **nearest enemy** and **kill them instantly** as well.

![[GOTChainAssassinate.png|500]]

---
#### Instant Kill

By holding **two specific input** (yet to be specified), the player will enter in a **flow state**, that will allow them to **target** up to 2 **enemies**, and **kill them instantly**.

This capacity **consumes health** of the player.

![[MiniNinjaPowerAttack.png|500]]

This capacity can be upgraded by defying the **[[LPOM-Narrative Design#Forgotten Swordsman|Forgotten Swordsman]]** in singular combat. Up to *5* **targets at maximum.**

---
