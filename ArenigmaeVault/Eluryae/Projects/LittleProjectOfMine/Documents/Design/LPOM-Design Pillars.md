## Adventure
The player of the project will be able to explore their map, find little secrets and side quests all around, it is not an Open World. 

It is a linear story, and as the map opens, as the player obtains new ways to navigate it, they will be able to go back to certain places they were not able to before.

## Puzzles

Enigma are a huge part of the project, the player must manipulate his environment and use his objects to traverse it.

This can and will be done in several ways, where each temple, wich serves as big puzzles/milestones, has a different mecanic/way to challenge the player.

## Heroism

The primary angle of the story is similar to Tolkien's ways of storytelling, the characters are driven by a force of nature that they deem good, not for their own interest. There is a defined general good and bad (although more subtle on a micro scale).

The combat and story will serve this idealism. The player is against horrible monsters, undead, cursed, demons...
