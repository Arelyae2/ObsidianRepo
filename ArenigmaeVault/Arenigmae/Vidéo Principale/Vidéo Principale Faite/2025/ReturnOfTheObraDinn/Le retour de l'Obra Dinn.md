# Histoire résumé 
## [I: Loose Cargo](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Loose_Cargo "Loose Cargo")

[![I2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/b/b1/I2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181024152534)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Loose_Cargo,_part_2 "Loose Cargo, part 2")

The Obra Dinn is at port in Falmouth, taking on cargo under the supervision of Danish seaman [Lars Linde](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Lars_Linde "Lars Linde"). A rope carrying cargo snaps, and the pallet falls, crushing another seaman, [Samuel Peters](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Samuel_Peters "Samuel Peters"). Unbeknownst to the crew, a stowaway hiding in one of the barrels also died from the fall.

## [II: A Bitter Cold](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/A_Bitter_Cold "A Bitter Cold")

[![II1](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/9/92/II1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181113160428)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/A_Bitter_Cold,_part_1 "A Bitter Cold, part 1")

As the Obra Dinn sails by the coast of Portugal, two Indian seamen, [Soloman Syed](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Soloman_Syed "Soloman Syed") and [Renfred Rajub](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Renfred_Rajub "Renfred Rajub"), succumb to a fatal lung disease, which they may have contracted in a lascar house.

Meanwhile, midshipmen [Thomas Lanke](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Thomas_Lanke "Thomas Lanke"), [Peter Milroy](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Milroy "Peter Milroy"), and [Charles Hershtik](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Charles_Hershtik "Charles Hershtik") assist the ship's butcher, [Emil O'Farrell](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Emil_O%27Farrell "Emil O'Farrell"), in slaughtering a cow for its meat.

## [III: Murder](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Murder "Murder")
[![III1](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/0/07/III1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181117121119)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Murder,_part_1 "Murder, part 1")

When the ship passes by the Canary Islands, Second Mate [Edward Nichols](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Edward_Nichols "Edward Nichols") secretly slips into the cargo hold. He knocks a guard, [Hok-Seng Lau](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Hok-Seng_Lau "Hok-Seng Lau"), unconscious and breaks into the room containing the Formosan chest. As Nichols is about to take the shell inside the chest, an Italian passenger, [Nunzio Pasqua](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Nunzio_Pasqua "Nunzio Pasqua"), wanders into the cargo hold and catches him in the act. His theft thwarted, Nichols stabs Pasqua to cover up his crimes.

Some time later, Captain [Robert Witterel](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Robert_Witterel "Robert Witterel") sentences Lau to death by firing line, claiming he had confessed. While this is never explained, [Li Hong](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Hong "Li Hong") was a member of Nichols's mutineers and fluent in Chinese, so it is plausible he deliberately mistranslated Lau's words as a confession. The Formosans protest Lau's execution, but to no avail. Meanwhile, [Edward Spratt](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Edward_Spratt "Edward Spratt"), the ship's artist, draws a sketch of the [execution scene](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Justice_at_Sea "Justice at Sea").

After the execution, Nichols organizes several seamen to help him steal the chest and abduct two of the Formosans. In the course of this escape, they attack several other crewmen. One topman, [Timothy Butement](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Timothy_Butement "Timothy Butement"), is shot dead as he attempts to stop the group.

## [IV: The Calling](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/The_Calling "The Calling")

[![IV1](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/3/35/IV1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181129045855)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/The_Calling,_part_1 "The Calling, part 1")

Nichols' band begins rowing their two boats towards the Canary Islands, but a group of hostile [mermaids](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Mermaid "Mermaid") attack the lifeboats, starting by spearing topman [Li Hong](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Hong "Li Hong") in the chest. Nichols takes cover on his boat, and nearly everyone else dies at the hands of the mermaids, with seamen [Patrick O'Hagan](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_O%27Hagan "Patrick O'Hagan") getting speared, [Aleksei Toporov](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Aleksei_Toporov "Aleksei Toporov") and [Alarcus Nikishin](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Alarcus_Nikishin "Alarcus Nikishin") being drowned, and passenger [Bun-Lan Lim](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Bun-Lan_Lim "Bun-Lan Lim") having her face clawed.

In the midst of the fight, passenger It-Beng Sia uses the spear that impaled Hong to break free from his bonds. He takes a knife from the bottom of the boat and stabs [Samuel Galligan](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Samuel_Galligan "Samuel Galligan"), Nichols' steward. Sia then takes the shell and places it inside the chest, creating bright beams that stun the mermaids, but the action burns his arm to the bone and costs him his life.

Nichols, the only survivor, hauls the unconscious mermaids onto the boats. As he approaches the Obra Dinn and hails the ship, passenger [Chioh Tan](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Chioh_Tan "Chioh Tan"), at this point the only surviving Formosan, shoots Nichols dead.

## [V: Unholy Captives](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Unholy_Captives "Unholy Captives")

[![V1](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/1/12/V1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181129045858)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Unholy_Captives,_part_1 "Unholy Captives, part 1")

The captured mermaids and the corpses of Nichols, Lim, and Sia are hauled aboard and Captain Witterel has seaman [Hamadou Diom](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Hamadou_Diom "Hamadou Diom") hold Tan for interrogation. With [Huang Li](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Huang_Li "Huang Li"), a Chinese topman, acting as an interpreter, Captain Witterel interrogates Tan about the death of Nichols, the mermaids, the chest, and the dead Formosans. Tan is only able to get out that the shell is dangerous yet must be protected before one of the captured mermaids shoots spikes through its netting, piercing him and Diom.

The mermaids, even as they are hauled down to the lazarette, prove dangerous. One of them slaps cook [Thomas Sefton](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Thomas_Sefton "Thomas Sefton") in the face with its tail, and seaman [William Wasim](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/William_Wasim "William Wasim"), one of the crewmen carrying the mermaid to the lazarette, falls down a flight of stairs, breaking his neck. After the mermaids are hastily secured in the lazarette, seaman [John Naples](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/John_Naples "John Naples") is implied to be tasked with guarding the door. [Fillip Dahl](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Fillip_Dahl "Fillip Dahl"), Captain Witterel's steward, is upset that the mermaids are being held captive and attempts to free them, attacking Naples and cutting off his leg, resulting in his death.

Captain Witterel, surprised that his "steward of twenty years" would do such a terrible thing, has Dahl tied up and hauled into the lazarette for his trouble.

## [VI: Soldiers of the Sea](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Soldiers_of_the_Sea "Soldiers of the Sea")

[![VI8](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/1/11/VI8.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181119131834)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Soldiers_of_the_Sea,_part_8 "Soldiers of the Sea, part 8")

After these events, the ship turns around and heads back to England. Shortly after the ship comes about, however, a storm strikes. Topman [Huang Li](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Huang_Li "Huang Li") is struck by lightning while working in the rigging. At the same time, two mysterious humanoid creatures mounted on giant crabs begin to board the ship from the sides. They attack and kill more of the crew while making their way to the belly of the ship – whether their agenda is to reach the shells or the mermaids is unclear. They spear topman [Nicholas Botterill](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Nicholas_Botterill "Nicholas Botterill") on the main deck, spike carpenter's mate [Marcus Gibbs](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Marcus_Gibbs "Marcus Gibbs"), and decapitate surgeon's mate [James Wallace](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/James_Wallace "James Wallace") and topman [Jie Zhang](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Jie_Zhang "Jie Zhang") on the Orlop Deck. Midshipman [Charles Hershtik](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Charles_Hershtik "Charles Hershtik"), one of the crewmen fighting the crab riders, manages to kill one by throwing a lantern at it, but he burns to death along with it. [Henry Evans](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Henry_Evans "Henry Evans"), the ship's surgeon, attempts to remove a spiked butcher O'Farrell from the wall, killing him in the process. The second crab rider is close behind. [Charles Miner](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Charles_Miner "Charles Miner") attempts to shoot it, but accidentally shoots the ship's steward, [Zungi Sathi](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Zungi_Sathi "Zungi Sathi"), who, unbeknownst to everyone, crawled behind a wall after being spiked earlier. The crew pursues the crab rider to the cargo deck, and [Winston Smith](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Winston_Smith "Winston Smith"), the ship's carpenter, shoots and kills the other crab rider with a blunderbuss as it spears him to death.

## [VII: The Doom](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/The_Doom "The Doom")

[![VII7](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/e/ec/VII7.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181027021201)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/The_Doom,_part_7 "The Doom, part 7")

Having survived the crab rider attack, three crewmen, seamen [Alexander Booth](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Alexander_Booth "Alexander Booth"), [Nathan Peters](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Nathan_Peters "Nathan Peters"), and purser [Duncan McKay](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Duncan_McKay "Duncan McKay"), decide to escape in one of the lifeboats. When seaman [Lars Linde](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Lars_Linde "Lars Linde") asks to join the group, Peters refuses and clubs Linde for killing his brother Samuel (in [Chapter I](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Loose_Cargo,_part_1 "Loose Cargo, part 1")), in spite of Linde and Booth's protests that the death was accidental.

West of Madeira, a [Kraken](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Kraken "Kraken") attacks the ship, causing the deaths of sixteen further crewmen, those being Artist Edward Spratt, who was strangled by the Kraken; seaman [Abraham Akbar](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Abraham_Akbar "Abraham Akbar"), who was crushed by the Kraken; seaman [George Shirley](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/George_Shirley "George Shirley"), who was pulled (or shot) out the window and drowned by the Kraken; Gunner [Christian Wolff](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Christian_Wolff "Christian Wolff"), who was shot with a cannon by the Kraken; Third Mate's steward [Roderick Andersen](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Roderick_Andersen "Roderick Andersen"), who was crushed by a loose cannon; Midshipman [Peter Milroy](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Milroy "Peter Milroy"), who was caught in an explosion; topman [Omid Gul](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Omid_Gul "Omid Gul"), who fell overboard; and topman [Maba](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Maba "Maba"), who was torn in half by the Kraken. Meanwhile those of the crew that had escaped in the lifeboat – seamen Nathan Peters, Alexander Booth, and purser Duncan McKay – all fell overboard and drowned when the Kraken flung their lifeboat into the air; the captain's wife, passenger [Abigail Hoscut Witterel](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Abigail_Hoscut_Witterel "Abigail Hoscut Witterel"), who stepped outside to look for her husband, was crushed by falling rigging; helmsman [Finley Dalton](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Finley_Dalton "Finley Dalton") and topman [Wei Lee](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Wei_Lee "Wei Lee") were pulled into the sea and drowned; and Bosun's mate Charles Miner was torn apart. The survivors after the attack made up only a skeleton crew.

## [VIII: Bargain](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Bargain "Bargain")

Inside the lazarette, Captain's steward Dahl breaks free from his handcuffs. He opens the chest and pulls out the shell from it, but he burns off his arm and dies, finding it full of an unknown substance that resembles quicksilver.

During the Kraken attack, Captain Witterel deduces that the mermaids are responsible, so he enters the lazarette and kills off two of them, in the hopes that they call off the attack. He takes two shells and possibly throws them overboard, but this is not fully confirmed in the narrative. Whether due to the shells or the last remaining mermaid calling off the Kraken, the attack stops, and the storm subsides.

Later, Third Mate [Martin Perrott](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Martin_Perrott "Martin Perrott") and stewards [Paul Moss](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Paul_Moss "Paul Moss") and [Davey James](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Davey_James "Davey James") enter the lazarette, only to come across the third shell in Dahl's hands. Perrott is spiked before he can assure the mermaid that he has come to set it free. Mortally wounded, he orders the two stewards to give the mermaid the shell, throw it overboard, and lock the door to the lazarette as they leave. He also asks the mermaid to see the Obra Dinn home.

Later still, Moss finds the ship's surgeon, Henry Evans, attempting to enter the lazarette when the key to it has been disposed of. Aware of the functionality of a [pocketwatch](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Pocketwatch "Pocketwatch") called the "Memento Mortem", and assuming that the East India Company would use the watch to investigate the ship should it ever make it back to port, Evans ties his pet [monkey](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Monkey "Monkey") to a rope, shoots it in the lazarette, and keeps its paw before leaving.

## [IX: Escape](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Escape "Escape")

[![IX3](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/obradinn/images/8/8d/IX3.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20181104105518)](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Escape,_part_3 "Escape, part 3")

Now north of Madeira, Fourth Mate [John Davies](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/John_Davies "John Davies") helps the dying Bosun, Alfred Klestil, to a chair in the gun deck. Davies tells him that his "Frenchman" was torn apart and that the Kraken went away with the storm, thanks to Captain Witterel.

Shortly after Klestil dies of severe blood loss, Gunner's Mate [Olus Wiater](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Olus_Wiater "Olus Wiater") expresses doubts of Captain Witterel's trustworthiness. He broaches the subject of mutiny with Davies, planning to take over the ship and sell the "wretched fish" and shells. Thomas Lanke, the only surviving midshipman at this point, overhears the conversation and panics, alerting the crewmen of mutiny. Wiater gives chase and stabs Lanke in the back.

Meanwhile on the main deck, Evans, James, Moss, and passengers [Emily Jackson](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Emily_Jackson "Emily Jackson") and [Jane Bird](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Jane_Bird "Jane Bird") attempt to leave the ship on the last remaining boat, but [Leonid Volkov](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Leonid_Volkov "Leonid Volkov"), a Russian topman, catches the group and attacks, getting into a sword fight with Moss. Despite the intervention of Captain Witterel, First Mate William Hoscut, seaman Henry Brennan, and topman [Lewis Walker](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Lewis_Walker "Lewis Walker"), Volkov stabs Moss, killing him. He proceeds to try and attack the others, but Jackson quickly shoots him down, killing him.

Unaware of the shot fired on the main deck, Davies attempts to stop Wiater from further harming the now mortally wounded Lanke, reaching for Wiater's gun. During the scuffle, the gun blows off Wiater's face. The dust-up catches the attention of First Mate [Hoscut](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/William_Hoscut "William Hoscut"), who rushes to the dying Lanke's aid, as seaman [Brennan](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Henry_Brennan "Henry Brennan") clubs Davies, wrongly believing that Davies killed Wiater intentionally.

As Hoscut tends to the dying Lanke, Lewis Walker throws Volkov's body overboard. The boat carrying Jackson, Bird, James, and Evans departs, and Captain Witterel slumps over, utterly defeated.

## [X: The End](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/The_End "The End")

After an indeterminate amount of time has passed, First Mate Hoscut, seaman Brennan, and topman Walker all turn on Captain Witterel and attempt to extort the shells from him, but he says he has thrown them all overboard. The crewmen do not buy this explanation and attack him. The Captain is forced to kill them all in self-defense. He shoots Hoscut with a gun, slits Brennan's throat, and clubs Walker in the head (who had already stabbed a knife into the captain's side).

After contemplation and overcome with grief, Captain Witterel sits beside his wife's body, lamenting the death of Hoscut, and asks for her forgiveness before shooting himself.

## Back In Falmouth

Five years later in 1807, the Obra Dinn unexpectedly drifts back to port at Falmouth. An East India Company insurance [inspector](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Inspector "Inspector") is summoned to determine what happened aboard the ship during its 5-year disappearance, and are given a [catalogue](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn_\(book\) "Return of the Obra Dinn (book)") and the pocketwatch, both of which surgeon Henry Evans mailed to the East India Company from Morocco. The sole mermaid watches from a distance on the water, having successfully seen the Obra Dinn home the past 5 five years.

The Inspector concludes their investigation and leaves the ship as a storm brews on the horizon. After leaving, the Obra Dinn promptly sinks in the storm. The Inspector then prepares a full insurance assessment for the East India Company and mails the book back to Henry Evans.

## London, A Year Later

Roughly a year later, surgeon Henry Evans dies in Africa due to illness, and one of the surviving passengers, [Jane Bird](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Miss_Jane_Bird "Miss Jane Bird"), sends the Inspector a [letter](https://obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/Endings "Endings") about how Evans felt about the Inspector's performance. The contents of this letter depend on how many fates the Inspector has correctly solved prior to their return.

If few fates were solved, the letter tells of Evans' severe dissatisfaction with the Inspector's results, and Bird notes that this depression prevented any recovery from his illness.

If many (but not all) fates were solved, the letter instead states that Evans was generally pleased with the Inspector's incomplete results, remarking that his final wish to see a completed book would be rather difficult, and for this he forgives the Inspector.

If all 58 solvable fates are completed, however, Bird instead sends the letter along with the catalogue back to the Inspector. The letter states that Evans was quite pleased with the Inspector's work, and asked that the catalogue be returned to the Inspector along with the means to complete it. The Inspector also receives a package containing the paw from Evans' monkey. Using the pocketwatch on the paw, the Inspector is transported to the previously inaccessible lazarette. Here, the Inspector fills out the remaining two fates. The Inspector keeps the catalogue, placing it on the shelf.

Avant d'en parler, on va faire une pause sur le jeu, puisque j'ai très envie de vous parler de son créateur.

Lucas Pope a commencé sa carrière chez Naughty Dog, ceux qui ont fait The Last Of Us ou Uncharted. Il quitte le studio en 2010, puis en 2013 sort son premier jeu, Paper Please. 

Ce jeu est incroyable, vous jouez un contrôleur de frontière dans un pays pseudo soviétique, et contrôlez les papiers des arrivant, les passeports, les carte, les permis de travail, faut détecter les informations voir si elles sont vrais ou fausse, y'a une intrigue avec des gens qui tentent de renverser le gouvernement, vous pouvez choisir de les aider ou non, ce jeu est génial, mais c'est pas une vidéo sur Papers Please.

Ce que je veux dire c'est que ce jeu est un succès, ce qui propulse donne a Lucas Pope un bon matelas financier pour faire un autre jeu, qui est cette fois Return of the Obra dinn. Et on est en 2014 à ce moment là.

Le jeu est sorti en 2018, 4 ans de développement, et c'était 4 ans intense. Imaginez faire un jeu de A a Z. Il faut créer les modèles, programmer les comportements, les visuels, ce look particulier style vieux jeu, il faut le coder ça aussi, ça représente des milliers d'heures de travail pour une seule personne.

Pendant le développement Lucas va faire une forme de burnout, si le jeu est aussi bien détaillé, aussi bien fourni, c'est parce que son développeur est atteint d'un perfectionniste quasi maladif, il avait personne pour déléguer le travail.

Dans une interview il a évoqué que la pire chose a faire, était la traduction. 
Une langue, même si elle a des origines similaires à une autre, a parfois des subtilités qui n'ont pas d'équivalent avec d'autres. Et quand tu fais un jeu qui te demande spécifiquement de faire des phrases très complexes, ça devient horrible.

Dans la version anglaise, pour cette personne, on dit clubbed. Qui décrit du coup un coup sur la tête par un objet lourd, mais en français, il n'y a pas d'équivalent direct, ou en tous cas, pas un équivalent qui permet de décrire la situation de manière autant fidèle sans rendre le joueur confus. Dans la version française, c'est coup de lance.

Le jeu est traduit en 14 langues, Français Anglais, Arabe, Russe, Ukrainien, Chinois Simplifié, Japonais, je vous les cite pas tous, mais il s'est pas rendu la tâche facile.

Cette aparté je tiens à la faire, parce que je veux souligner l'incroyable travail de Lucas Pope sur ce jeu, ce qu'il a fait est incroyable.

Ce jeu, au travers d'un excellent gameplay, nous raconte une tragédie provoqué par la cupidité des hommes. Mais, si j'enlève le gameplay, cette histoire elle est pas si intéressante que ça.

Dans Return Of The Obra Dinn, l'histoire, c'est le Gameplay, et donc la façon dont on reconstitue celle de l'équipage.
