## Enemy behavior

![[EnemyPatrol.png|350]]
All enemies are either:
- **Waiting** on a **defined point**
- **Walking** around in a **delimited zone**
- **Walking** on defined **patrol point**

They have several zone of detection:
- Afar - A **huge cone in their vision** - they go to the latest player position.
  
  If the player is **still there**, they are **naturally detected**, if not, they **look around**, and then go back to the **closest asked point of patrol/waiting/walking**
  
- Close - A **cone** in their vision - the player is **instantly detected**.
  
- Intimate - A **circle around them** - the player is **instantly detected**

## Enemies

The enemies will challenge the player on their different skills and objects.
### Grounded Combat

 All have **at least** two type of moves:

- **Standing hit**
- **Running into hit**

#### Hollows 
Hollows are **not undead**, but rather living beings that **succumbed to madness or diseases**. They now wander without precise purpose, but will **attack anyone they see**.

The player will encounter [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Humans|Humans]] hollows and [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Alfiryans|Alfiryans]] hollows.

![[Hollow.png|350]]

They are very slow enemies and only the **base set of moves** with a rusty piece of metal.

The [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Alfiryans|Alfiryans]] can deal a bit **more damage** and have **more health**.

---

There is a version of hollows that can **fire a bow** to the player, they do **not have the base move**, only a bow.
#### Undead Malenaian

Undead [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Malenaians|Malenaians]] are generally **travelers that adventured** too much in cursed territories like the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Great Eluryian Forest|Great Eluryian Forest]] or the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Lost Mountains|Lost Mountains]] and got killed.

Their bodies then **rose up from the dead** and wandered around, **killing everything in sight**. It is only their body, their soul being already collected by [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Death|Death]].

![[UndeadGiant.png|350]]

They are **slow but very powerful**, in addition to the base move, they also possess:
- **Slap to reject the player** away from them.
- A **follow up to their base** move.

#### Undead Alfiryan

Undead [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Alfiryans|Alfiryans]] are generally **soldiers** that separated from their squads in cursed territories like the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Great Eluryian Forest|Great Eluryian Forest]] or the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Lost Mountains|Lost Mountains]] and got killed.

Their bodies then **rose up from the dead** and wandered around, **killing everything in sight**. It is only their body, their soul being already collected by [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Death|Death]].

![[UndeadTiefling.png|250]]

They are **fragile but fast**, still equipped with their weapons
In addition to their base move
- **Two to three follow up** to the hit
- A **jump hit**, their weapons is then stuck to the ground.

---
There is a version of Undead [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Alfiryans|Alfiryans]] that can **fire a bow** to the player, they do **not have the base move**, only a bow.
#### Pyrofeu Soldier

The [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Pyrofeu|Pyrofeu]] soldiers are a hostile race during the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#First Act|First Act]] of the game, they become **friendly after the last dungeon** of the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#First Act|First Act]].

![[LordOfNightArmorGOT.png|200]]

The Pyrofeu are equipped of **heavy obsidian armor**, making them **invulnerable to most attack**, even of a gun, 
An armor **can** be taken off, by striking if they are **stunned**, or **behind their back**.

They exist as **several variations**:

 - **Sword** - A two handed  sword, making them slow
- **Double Swords** - More movement, but less resistance
- **Spear** - More range but less movements
- **No Weapons** - Very fast but ont powerful

After the end of the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#First Act|First Act]], they become friendly.

These can be either [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Alfiryans|Alfiryans]], [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Malenaians|Malenaians]], [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Pyrofeu|Pyrofeus]], or [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Humans|Humans]].

![[KarlachCliffgate.png|250]]

#### Corrupted Phantom

A living being completely overtaken by the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Golden Corruption|Golden Corruption]], even their body. Making them **weak, but fast** in combat.

![[melCorruptedBlackRose.png|250]]

They can also send **projectiles of tar** to the player.

#### Yellow Guardian

Yellow Guardians are watchers that are **not very powerful** but alert others around.

![[GoldenCorruption_1 1.png|250]]

They have their **basic attack** but can also **ressucitate other dead infected**.

#### Knight of the Corruption

The final form of a living being overtaken by the [[LPOM-Narrative Design#Golden Corruption|Golden Corruption]], they are equipped of a **Golden long sword** and a **shorter** one.

![[KnightsOfTheCorruption.png|250]]

They are **extremely fast** but almost **robotic like** in their movement, 

In addition to their base move
- **Three to Four follow up** to the base hit
- **A second combo** for the player to avoid
- A **jump hit**, their weapons is then stuck to the ground.

Bats will be waiting on the **roof of caves** and temples, and attack the player **in group**, one at a time.

![[bats.png|350]]

The only have a **dash attack**, and can be **easily killed**.

![[BooSuperMarioBros.png|100]]

### Big Enemy

#### Amygdala

Human sized spider are **fixed on the wall** and **jump on the player** if they don't pay attention to their **shadows on the ground**.

They can throw **web ball** to make the player **fix in place** and attack them.

![[GiantHouseSpider.png|350]]

They can go on walls and roof.
#### Crocodile

Crocodile hunt **alone**, once spotting a player, they will **slowly go towards them**, either by **swimming or walking** when the player is not looking.

If the player **looks at them**, they will **immobilise** and wait.

If the player **attack before the crocodile do**, they will go on a **killing frenzy**.

![[CrocodileMarin.png|450]]

They can be quite **tanky**, but their **tail is their weakness**.

#### Lava golem

Lava golem are a **huge tank of enemy**, they **cannot be killed**, only **tired out** and then **torn apart** by **cutting their limb**.

![[LavaElementalBG3.png|350]]
They have **slow** but **powerful attack** patterns that **stuck them in the grounds**.

#### Wild Asmodens

Asmodens are a wild species that are naturally violent. A wild Asmodens is made to be fought with another Asmodens.

![[TibetanMastiff.png|350]]

Without a mount, the player will have to **flee them**, or be extremely prudent and **evade quickly**.

They charge a prey to **knock them down** and **stomp them** with their paw.
On another Asmodens, both **mount charge**, and a [[#Quick Time Event]] launches to determine who wins.

Once a wild Asmodens is down, the player can **stomp them** with their **own Asmodens** and kill them in a **few hits**.
### Special Enemy

Special enemies are foes that requires a **certain object to be defeated or weakened**, or have a **gimmick**.

#### [[LPOM-Narrative Design#The Sentinels|Sentinels]]

Sentinels are extremely **fast** and **agile foes** they will **easily avoid sword** hit and **even bullets**.

![[WendigoSecondUntilDawn.png|463]]

When **they jump**, the player has to **shoot at sentinels very quickly**, **knocking them** down and making them **extremely vulnerable** to a few sword hit.

If the payer is **not quick enough**, a [[#Quick Time Event]] will launch to get the sentinel **out of the player's back**.
#### Shielded Enemy

This variation is a gimmick that can be applied to the [[#Corrupted Living]], [[#Hollows]], [[#Pyrofeu Soldier]], and [[#Undead Alfiryan]].

![[HugeSoldierShield.png|250]]

They will be equipped of a **shield protecting them of any front attack**, the shield can be **easily removed** with the [[LPOM-Player Design#Grapling Hook|Grapling Hook]].

Without the [[LPOM-Player Design#Grapling Hook|Grapling Hook]], the player can still kill them by going **behind their back**, but very difficultly.
#### Invisible Enemy

A special ghost that **follows the player around** with their **weapons** (sword, spear...).

They only reveal themselves and **become physical when preparing to attack**, but they will **flee** if the player tries to **attack them before they reveal themselves**.
![[PoeGhostZeldaTP.png|250]]

#### Fear enemy

A **mummified undead** enemy that will **act as statues** when **looked at by the player**.
When the player is **not looking**, they will **run toward them extremely** quickly.

![[ZeldaEffroi.png|250]]

They will attack the player **once they are close enough**, and reveal themselves.

## Scaling System

Each enemy can have **harder variants** with **more health and attack speed** and combo...

During certain **events** of the game, naturally by **killing enemies**, or obtaining more **health point**, a **passive system** will add XP to an overall count.

The **next enemies** that are spawned will naturally be a **bit harder for the player**.
