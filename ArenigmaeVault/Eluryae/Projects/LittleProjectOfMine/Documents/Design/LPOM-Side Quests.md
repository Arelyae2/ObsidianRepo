## Collectible
### Rare silver coin

 **Basic** coin - 1 unit (damaged, missing a piece)
- **Freshly** coin - 5 unit (solid copper coin)
- **Honored** coin - 10 unit (copper coin with some ornaments)
- **Precious** coin - 20 unit (copper coin with gems)
- **Silvery** coin - 50 unit (copper coin with gems and silver)
-  **Ornamented** coin - 100 unit (beautifully ornamented silver and copper coin with gems on the sides)
-  **Silver** coin - 200 unit (a fully silver coin with diamonds on the side)

The player can sometimes find rare iterations of each tiers of the currency, when looting any of them, they can either sell them for a higher price, or keep them co complete the collection and win another reward (to determine).
They can obviously have multiples example of it.

When collecting a rare coin, then loot the normal amount of money on it, and the coin as well, they can then sell it for even more money (this is paradoxical but better in UX)

There is also a shop where the player can buy lootboxes with their money, and maybe find rare currencies (so pack opening like).
