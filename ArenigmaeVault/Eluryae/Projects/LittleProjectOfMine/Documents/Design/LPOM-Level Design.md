## World

## Temples
Non linear:
- Choice of where to spend a small key
- One big central room that splits out
- Choice in events of the dungeon

Linear temple:
- Recontextualisation of a linear path with an object
- Backtracking

- Action in a room has consequences in another

What is important is the atmosphere of each temple, and a slow build-up of the music until the final boss of it.

## Golden Corruption

A mix of Infiltration and Mazes formed by the architecture of the regions.

### The Corrupted Forest

Main possessions:
- Squirrel
- Wolf

Catalysts:
- Corrupted tree
- Corrupted rabbit

Infiltration & Maze like, going up in the trees branches to avoid the sentinels above.

Chase sequence (poppy playtime like with a sentinel, no death in case of getting caught, put in a cage with other dead squirrels, easy escape to go back).

![[HugguWuggyChasePoppyPlaytime.png|400]]

When falling off the trees, the player will not be captured immediately by a sentinel, and have a chance to escape.
