## UX Ideas

- There is a hint system for the player to be able to activate and deactivate.
- Same for the tutorial, but both system are separated.

- The objects can be equipped quickly by assigning shortcuts, a second screen could be added as well (twilight princess pad like ?)

- Save system is automatic at all major steps + every x time, but the player can manually save.
- Save every x time must be only in certain situations/zone

- Collectibles must be a mean to a better end than just collect them (coins is casino and makes the player rich, catalyst leads to challenges and mini temples, easter eggs are exploration and non obligated to complete 100%...)

- Making of in the menu, music in the menu (with intensity settings and all)

- Si un joueur n'a pas a joué depuis un certain temps, lui proposer un rappel des mécaniques de combat.
