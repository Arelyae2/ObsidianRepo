#### Forgotten Swordsman

The Forgotten Swordsman is a character that the player will meet after the first temple.

He is dressed in an **aristocratic human attire**, **equipped of a cane and a metallic red mask** hiding his face, he is **always sitted on a chair.**

![[RenoirClairObscur.png|200]]

His function is to teach the [[LPOM-Player Design#Secret Moves|Secret Moves]] to the player, by spawning **animated skeletons**.

---

He mysteriously **appears in every town and cities the player visits**, even sometimes ruins, **playing dice alone or with someone else**.

---

After the last **Secret Move** learned by the layer, the Swordsman will **propose the player a more difficult challenge**.

If the player accepts, the swordsman will then **get up and reveal that he never needed a cane**, only **hiding a sword in it**. He will also reveal to be **Death** itself.
##### Death

Death is **merely observing the universe**, they are **more powerful than** everything that could exist.

*"Even the greater one will not get hold of me, in fact, i will before they hope to attain true immortality."*

They do not have any **particular purposes other than collecting souls** of dead, and being immortal, **they do not need to kill anyone, just wait**, even encouraging peace and prosperity.

The **rise of the Golden Corruption frustrated death**, as it forbid them of their purpose, the Golden Corruption **destroying the souls it touches**.

They do **not want to help directly** the player, but **train them** to defeat the Golden Corruption, and **reestablish the status quo** of nature.

#### Yellow King

The Yellow King is the leader of the **fanatics** of the [[#Golden Corruption]] cult.

He is a **human** **presenting, and even seeing himself**, as a horror beyond the comprehension of everyone, hiding his face behind a **huge cloak and a skull mask made of gold**.

![[YellowKing.png|300]]

His **disguises** and lies show a huge **insecurity** and need to put other down to elevate himself.

---

He is the one **using the artifact** stole from the citadel to summon **what he believes** is the [[#Golden Corruption]] true power, but is actually a door to the [[#Nine Hells]].

![[YellowKing2.png|300]]

He will there meet the [[#Golden King]], and receive power from him. **He believes** that the **Golden King true intent** is to take the **Golden Corruption to himself**, he then tries to gain as much power for himself as possible in Elury.

---

His goal given by the Golden King is to **corrupt** the [[#Heir of the Corruption]], to fully **awaken the old magic** of the Golden Corruption.

He will be **searching him for the first act of the game**. And will realise that it is the player at the end of it.

![[GCLiqui.png|400]]

He will then try to **corrupt the player**, but they will be saved by their **Greater Ones ancestry**, **escaping the Yellow King**.

---

He will fight the player during certain parts of the game, using mainly Golden corruption **magic** and **daggers**.

![[OldDagger.png]]

At the **beginning of the third act**, he will be **defeated by the player**, once he is near to death, his disguises and lies revealed, he will **beg the player for mercy**, and tell him the intentions of the Golden King.

Whatever the player decides, the Golden King takes **physical form** by **getting out of the body of the Yellow king, violently killing him** in the process.

##### History

The Yellow King was born from an aristocratic family in [[#Eluryae the Great]]. 

He took **interest in literature and politics** at a **young age**. Being **good with words**, he decided to change the **world for the best**, he was sent on an espionage mission of the fanatics of the [[#Golden Corruption]]. As they tempted to stole old artifacts from the city's library.

At first only here to do **reconnaissance and infiltration**, he got himself into the sect as a **willing participant**, **brainwashed** in mere months. Now starting to **spy for them**. 

His charisma leading him **upwards the ranks**, until he became **their leader**. His desire for good twisted into a **nihilistic need** of *cleansing* the world.

#### Golden King

The Golden King is the **leader of the [[#True Order]]**, he has no origins other than being **born of hate and pure power**. He serves as the **final boss** of the game.

![[GoldenKing.png|400]]

---

**Before** he became this form, he was one of the [[#Greater Ones]], **overseeing** the [[#Nine Hells]], by **observing the same state of war and massacre**, he **feared** that it would extend to other dimensions.

![[GreaterOnes1.png|250]]

As the concept of **fear did not even exist in the Nine Hells, only hate, the Golden Corruption present in the** [[#Nine Hells]] **quickly overtook him easily**, concentrating it's full power.

He descended, **taking an humanoid form**, and became the Golden King.

---

He rules over the [[#True Order]], one day, a **portal appeared  in his own castle** by the means of a **forgotten magic in his realm**.

![[Pandemonium.png|400]]

He realises that **magic is nearly gone in the realm of Elury**, **defenseless** against any potentiel attack, he decides to give to the [[#Yellow King]] some of his own powers to control the [[#Golden Corruption]].

---

His true intents are to **rule over this new discovered realm**, but he realises that the **magic of the [[#Greater Ones]] is still present**, and that a **child** between the **Golden Corruption** and Them exists in the realm, the [[#Heir of the Corruption]]

The Heir needs to be corrupted, so that the Golden King will **assimilate them**.

In the game, he **doesn't appear or is mentioned** by anyone until the [[#Second Act]] of the game

---

He **fully appears before the player** during the end of the [[#Third Act]] by getting **out of the body** of the [[#Yellow King]] (killing him in the process).

He **assimilates the control** of the [[#Golden Corruption]], and releases the power of the True Order, and by proxy, **magic back into the Eluryian Universe**.

---

During the final fight, realizing that the player will **inevitably defeat him**, he chooses to completely **abandon the rest of his Greater One side to the Golden Corruption**, losing his reason, but **becoming the Golden God**.

Out of **pure pettiness** towards the **Greater Ones** and the player.

![[GodOfGold.png|300]]

In this form, he has **no true will other than his last thought**;  **destroying the player** and **ruling over the rest of all Universes**.

---

After he is **truly defeated and killed**, the rest of his soul will disperse, he **isn't confirmed to be dead, only sealed away**.

##### Adventure Calls

###### The Death of the Yellow King

The player disarms the [[#Yellow King]] completely, and is about to kill him, when the Yellow King suddenly starts to **beg**.

Crawling away, he takes off his **mask and cloak**, revealing a **simple human** behind, trying to talk his way out of the situation he got himself into.

*I'm a simple beggar, like you, those people... I... I didn't want to do this, they made me, i swear, i swear to whatever god you want me to, i conjure your to let me live, please, please... I... Ugh, ugh...*

As he talks, he has **trouble breathing**, more and more, trying to find words to explain himself, the breathing starts to **wheeze**, then he **chokes** on what appears to be nothing, he falls on his arms.

![[StarWarsForceChoke.png|410]]

The lights of the room **dims out, as winds start to rush in**, the man has more and more trouble breathing, suddenly, he stares at the roof, and screams in pain. The **scream is immediately shut** down by a **mass growing from his mouth**, and suddenly sprouts out of his now dislocated jaw, a **hand**, then an entire arm.

A second hand opens the **rest of his jaw** and finishes to **silence him**. As the rest of the person gets out of the now dead [[#Yellow King]]. The [[#Golden King]].

![[GoldenKing.png|289]]

*Two hundred, twenty eight thousand, one hundred and thirty seven years, as i have waited for such an occasion. It is not a lowly wench like this one that will stop me. My impatience must be quenched.*

He looks at the player.

*You are a blasphemy, even for the likes of me.*

And cast a **powerful unknown magic**, opening a **rift in reality**, the player is sucked in it.

*I will take more than pleasure of torturing the expression of their desperation, as for the rest of you,  march towards the capital*

The player is now in the [[#Nine Hells]], he sees monsters around him, imps and demon, ignoring him and going towards a gigantic palace in the distance, the home of the [[#True Order]].

### Alfiryans

The Alfiryans are a race of horned amazons that thrives in war and dance.
They inhabit the **Canyons of Elury**.

![[KarlachCliffgate.png|300]]
![[GrandCanyon.png|300]]
#### Physicality

Long descendants of the [[#Nine Hells]], the Alfiryans met **during the events of the game** are **all of female gender**, they were **once male Alfiryans**, but **too long ago to even remember the concept** of it. 

Making pronouns trivial to use, the Alfiryans will always **prefer to use their name and surnames**, seeing **pronouns as archaic**.

They are born with **horns**, and a **skin** going from **Dark Red/Blue to Light Ocre/Purple as they age**.
Their **horns varies greatly** in range.

![[TieflinfHorns.png|400]]

Their height go **from 1.8 meters to 2.5 at maximum (without horns)**.

They **do not need a male part to reproduce**, and have developed a way to create children via **cloning and mixing their genes** (there is history of Alfiryans leaving the tribe to form a family out of the town with others, or even humans).

Their culture being concentrated on combat and dance, they are naturally **muscular**.

They live up to **100 years at their oldest**.

#### Culture

Alfiryans are a **species respectful** of others. Welcoming other visitors, but quickly protective of themselves when necessary.

They send ambassadors around the world to **spread their culture** and sell their **services as mercenaries**.

However, they keep **certain part of their cities closed to non Alfiryans**, as a way to secure their scientific and cultural secrets.

##### War
Most of the Alfiryans are formed in the arts of acrobatic combat, it is classed in two types, **Defensive** and **Offensive**.

Each category has it's own style as well:

- Two handed
- Double sword
- Single handed
- Sword and Shield
- Double blade on a spear
- etc...

A **squad** of Alfyrian warriors is equally composed of both the **defensive** and **offensive** type, 6 warriors in total.

![[Vibadass.png|500]]

All Alfiryans warriors cut their hair to not be distracted when fighting. They generally dye them with **black and enemy blood**.

---

Their armor is made of **metal and leather**, bought from [[#Malenaians]], the same is done for their weapons.

The pacific culture of the [[#Malenaians]] forced the Alfiryans to decorate their armor and weapons themselves, and **they excel** at it, decorating their **leather with silver and brass**, and **weapons with copper**.

The armors are made to be **light** for dynamic fighting, but it doesn't forbid a **mask**, a **helmet** or even **horns protections** (making them even more dangerous in combat).

Some **parts** can be **fully metallic** (shoulder, arms, thighs...)

![[Ambessa.png|300]]

They generally **lose one horn**, or even both, as **they use it in combat**, along their lives, if they can, **they keep the severed horns on their armor** as a souvenir of their dedication and strength.

![[Horns.png|500]]

---

During **great times of war**, or when doing a **parade**, the Alfiryans will do a **war march**, with every squads walking in formation.

In the formation, the squads at the **back will play music**, the squads **inside** will **watch** around for threat, and the **side and front squads will wave** the enormous **Alfyrian flag**.

![[MilitaryParade.png|500]]

A tactic used to **impress enemy and allies alike**.

##### Dance
Another part of the Alfiryans, or **retired warriors**, take part in the main entertainment of their race, **dance**. 
Heavily **linked with combat**, the best **warriors are generally good dancers** as well.

![[AbiLaGoat.png|200]]
![[MaghlaRef.jpg|200]]

They take part, late at night, in **hypnotic dances** and singing to tell stories to **other races or even their counterpart**.
[Example/Inspiration dance ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UdQshRbiX0Q)

[Example/Inspiration chant](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2FcZwsbmq3A)

This art is **spreaded across the world**, the Alfyrian dancers even collecting stories from other species.

They use **Bagpipes** and **heavy drums** during **war march**.
And a combination of **deep throat** singing from the older ones, and **lighter** from the youth when dancing.

%%Atreides house style%%

---

The dancers wear a lot of **fabric to create shadows** and images. They also let their **hair grow out** more, and **decorate their horns** with jewelery and metal.

![[TieflinfJewelry.png|308]]

##### Science

Alfyrian scientist are the most **secret part of their species**, they are the ones permitting the others to thrive and having no need of male.

Staying in the **enclosed part of the city**, they have a cover story of what they do when getting out of it, even to other Alfiryans.

---

They create **fertilized embyros** from others cells (while keepind the genetic diversified), and **give them to the public with abundance**, as a way to not create a limit of possible Aflyrians.

It is said that procreation was much easier before with the use of magic, but is now a forgotten art and work.

---

An Aflyrian couple can either **give their own cells to create an embyros**, or be given one. 
The rest of the process is then at the **discretion of the couple**.

Alfyrian parents are **obligated to stay in the city**, and **taken closely care of up until the child is 5 years old** they can then return to their job.

---
##### Asmodens

Alfyrian warriors, and travelers, when sending squad or dancers around the world, can either do it on foot, or by **mounting Asmodens they ride, even to combat**.

The **Asmodens** are a breed of **giant dog the size of a horse** when at full maturity, they live up to **60 years**.

![[TibetanMastiff.png|300]]

Only **elite warriors** or trained travelers can truly control an **Asmoden**, but a fully sized mount can welcome **up to 4 people**.

![[TibetanMastiffPuppy.png|200]]

---
##### Hierarchy

The Alfiryans hierarchy is made of a council of 4 Mistresses that reunite every week:
- The Mistress of the **Army** - **Elected** every year by the population.
- The Mistress of the **Forge** - The most skilled blacksmith **until death**
- The Mistress of the **Dance** - The most skilled dancer **until death**
- The Mistress of **Science** - **Randomly selected** every reunion.

Every reunion can be attended **by the rest of the population** as well.

### Pyrofeu

The Pyrofeu are a species of very offensive **humanoid lava fish** living in the heart of the **Eluryian Volcano**.

![[FighterFish.png|300]]
![[Axolotl.png|300]]

#### Physicality
The Pyrofeu are **humanoid fishes** that still bear most of their animalistic characteristics, allowing them to swim, but in lava.

![[ZorasOOT.png|200]]

Their **skin is of a very deep red, almost rubis** like, their **fully black eyes** allows them to see in the dark.

---

They live up to **50 years at their oldest**, a naturally very short lived species that slowly progresses in science to live longer.

They live in the [[#Great Eluryian Volcano]], they can **swim in magma naturally**, and live on the shore of the lava lakes and river.

#### Culture

The Pyrofeus are an **extremely defensive species**, knowing that the volcano was previously mined by the [[#Malenaians]] in the past, they are aggressive towards them and their allies the [[#Alfiryans]], an accord of peace forbid them to not launch a full on war.

Among themselves, they can be **cruel and violent, even mortal to their child** when forming them to the ways of combat (Githyanki like).

#### Ressources

Inside the volcano, the Pyrofeus mine mostly deep black obsidian, and sell it (reluctantly) mostly to [[#Malenaians]], who in exchange, give tools and metal.

![[Obsidian.png|300]]

They also **hunt lava fish** and **stone iguanas** to eat and sustain.

---

With the black obsidian, they also **craft weapons**, and **armor** that they sculpt to **resemble dragon scale**.

Their **armor fully cover** them when on the ground, **retaining** a burning **heat** to maintain them alive.

![[LordOfNightArmorGOT.png|300]]

When at **home**, they only keep a **light armor on the shoulders and hips** to stay agile.

#### War

Their most prominent culture is **combat**, a very **agressive style of attack**. **Ignoring defense and pain** to win no matter what.

![[ObsidianWeapon-1.png|300]]

### Merovlogiens
The Merovlogiens are a species of humanoid  birds that lives in the [[#Great Eluryian Rivers]] 

![[KingFisher.png|400]]

#### Physicality
Merovlogiens are **humanoid birds** with the biggest **inspiration being the kingfishers**.
Their average size when fully adult is **2 to 3 meters**.

They **cannot fly, their wings being not powerful enough**, but can glide on the air and the surface of the water, see [[#Hunt]]

They live up until **120 years at their oldest**.

They hatch eggs.

#### Hunt
The Merovlogiens hunt by **gliding above** the surface of the **water**, paying attention to not touch it, once they sport their prey, **they glide and catch it in their mouth**.

![[KingfisherHunting.png|200]]

They then kill it by **transpercing it with their knife**, and bring it ashore. An experienced Merovlogiens can get **up until 5 fish in a single dive**.

**Youngers** Mervologiens learn to dive first by staying on **high ground before gliding**.
#### Nomads

The Merovlogiens live on the **shores of the Great Eluryian Rivers**, they are a **nomad species** that travel along water bodies.

![[FishVillage.png|300]]

They settle by **anchoring their floating houses on the shore**, and put the necessary in the trees.

The Merovlogiens try **not to stay too long in the same spot**, staying only if the younglings are too young to support the balancing of the houses.

In general, they do a full circle along the rivers in 5 years.

---

This constant movement makes them **hard to spot**, this also makes them quite **knowledgeable of the rivers** of Elury.

They are **not a combative species**, but can be **extremely efficient if killing is necessary**, by hiding in the trees or water, and using the **same technique when hunting**.

### The Sentinels

The sentinels are a tribe of humanoids cannibalistic monsters that lives in the [[#Great Eluryian Forest]].

![[SentinelsV1.png|200]]
#### Physicality

##### Origins

The sentinels were one of the **first human tribe** that landed on the shores of the Eluryian Continent, they quickly **separated from the rest** of their species, and **installed a village** on the borders of what will be the [[#Great Eluryian Forest]].

After an attempt from **another settlements to makes them disappear**, they **resented** the rest of the settlers, and disappeared directly in the forest.

But their lack of knowledge of the forest made it impossible for them to **hunt and feed**, hence, they resorted to **hunt and eat their own comrades**, then other humans.

This way of life **transformed them** in the generations, along with **incest and bloodlust**.
Their biggest mutation came with the **rise and fall of magic in ancient Elury**, finally transforming them into a **complete monstrous species**.

![[Monsters.png|271]]

They became **fast and agile**, their arms became as long as their already **elongated legs**

![[WendigoUntilDawn.png|300]]

Their **skin** became **translucent**, and their face disappeared in the profit of **large and extensible jaw** holding **more teeth** than nature usually allows.
##### Today

The Sentinels are monsters that can measure up to **2.5 meters** when fully extended on their legs, with **translucent white skin** and **little white eyes** shining in the dark.

#### Culture
The Sentinels take their name for their extremely protective nature of the deep parts of the Great Forest, hunting anyone intruding their land.

Inside their caverns, the player will be able to find hands spray painted on the walls, and representation of them, seemingly proving a form of intelligence.

![[HandCavePrehistoric.png|330]]

##### Cannibalism

They usually move **fast and quietly** in the forest, completely **staying still and disappearing** in shadows.

Once they **spot** someone, they will **follow it quietly**, hiding behind trees and bushes. Once they **reveal** themselves or get seen, they go on a **full rampage**, and will pursue their prey **without care for discretion**, killing it with their own hands.

They will then butcher, cook, and eat their prey in their settlements.

![[Painting1.jpg|300]]

##### Communication

Their way of life made them **regressed** to the **incapacity of complex thoughts**, but they still communicate with each other, and created their own **traditions** and standards.

![[WickedDances.png|200]]

They notably use **dance** as a form of distraction, and communicate in a **harsh clicking and guttural language with simple words**.

##### Religion
They pray to what they believe is **an outer god they call the "Harbinger"**, they offer sacrifices to it, praying to live another day.

The "Harbinger" is actually a **long living representation of the [[#Golden Corruption]]**, and feast on the sacrifices infused with the fear of the sentinels from it.

![[LovecraftianHorror.png|494]]

### Humans

Humans are **humans**...

![[HumansFinalFantasy.png]]
#### Technology
The human technology in the Eluryian world is comparable to **medieval** times. 

![[HyruleCastleTown.png|400]]
Living either in a **city around a castle**, or smaller **villages**.

![[OrdonVillage.png|400]]

They are the only race that can use **firepowder and canons**.

They **established the main trade routes in Elury**, and are the **author of most of the agriculture and breeding techniques**.

#### Culture

The humans are the **dominating species of Elury**, their technological advancement gave them a huge advance on the others species.

![[AgricultureHumans.png]]

Their main city is at the **far East** of Elury, in the grand city of [[#Eluryae the Great]], a castle surrounded by a small city, sitting on a gigantic mountain chain. 

This main city communicates with other villages via trade routes.

#### Other Species

Humans are welcoming the other species to their cities, but interfere in their culture.
- Trying to quietly take **control of the Malenaians mines**.
- Get the **knowledge of the Alfiryans scientist.**
- Getting the Merovlogiens to **settle to watch them closely**.

While still trying to stay as hard power allies.

They are also in a **political conflict against the Pyrofeus** since they refused to let the humans mine obsidian off their volcano.

#### Hierarchy

The humans are ruled by a **Regent**.

## General Trivia

- The word **"Madame"** or **"Sir"** and their derivatives, **do not exist in the world** of Elury. People refer to other as **"Master" or "Mistress"** (according to the gender) followed by their name. 
  
	Example: "*Master* Ema, i am Master Manuel, i am pleased to meet you."
	
	Here, **Ema is referred as Master**, as **they do not refer to themselves as Male nor Female**, this is an **[[#Alfiryans]] exception**. The Alfyrians being entirely female, they have the **liberty to choose their gender** during their life.


- The term **"Magic", is used as both**, the **inexplicable manipulation** of elements, and now in the "modern world", as what we would call **science**.
